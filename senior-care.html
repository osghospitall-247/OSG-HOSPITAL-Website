Planned Future Initiative

OSG Senior Care — Old Age Home

A Future Home for Comfort, Dignity and Companionship

At OSG Hospital, we understand how important it is for senior citizens to have a comfortable living environment, respectful support, and assistance with everyday activities when needed. We are planning to introduce an Old Age Home and Senior Citizen Care service for families seeking accommodation and daily living support for their elderly loved ones.

Proposed Facilities

Proposed Senior Care Facilities & Amenities

Our proposed service is intended to focus on comfort, dignity, personal care, and a supportive living environment for elderly individuals.

Proposed Facility

1. Senior Citizen Accommodation

Plans for a comfortable residential environment designed around the physical safety, accessibility, and comfort needs of older adults, with barrier-free movement and peaceful living quarters.

Proposed Facility

2. Meal Arrangements

Plans to provide regular, nutritious meals for residents, with specific dietary requirements, hygiene, and wholesome balance considered according to final service arrangements.

Proposed Facility

3. Assistance with Daily Living

Plans to offer assistance with everyday routines, mobility, hygiene, and compassionate companionship, tailored to residents' individual needs and level of independence.

Our Care Philosophy

Our Approach to Elderly Care

We believe older adults deserve respect, understanding, and the opportunity to maintain their dignity and independence.

As we develop our planned senior care service, our aim is to understand the real needs of senior residents and their families, establishing appropriate care arrangements that provide genuine peace of mind.

  • Respect for privacy, choices, and personal dignity
  • Supportive living atmosphere fostering companionship and social interaction
  • Active and transparent communication with family members and caregivers
  • Proximity to hospital General Medicine consultations and clinical guidance
Important Planning Status Advisory

Planning to Care for Your Loved Ones?

If you are exploring senior citizen accommodation and assisted care options in Bengaluru, you may contact OSG Hospital to enquire about future plans and updates.

Please note: The Old Age Home is currently in the planning stage. Accommodation, meals, and daily living assistance are proposed services and are not yet advertised as operational. Admission dates, fees, location, staffing, and final facilities have not yet been confirmed. We do not advertise 24/7 emergency care, dementia units, or round-the-clock intensive medical supervision unless formally established.
