Confirmed Medical Care

Medical Services at OSG Hospital

OSG Hospital focuses on General Medicine, inpatient bed facilities, and nursing care. Our aim is to provide patients with an appropriate care environment and support based on their individual needs.

Primary Clinical Focus

1. General Medicine

General Medicine focuses on the clinical assessment and management of common adult health concerns, seasonal ailments, and chronic conditions.

Patients can enquire about medical consultations, thorough evaluation of symptoms, tailored treatment guidance, lifestyle management, and follow-up arrangements. Our physicians take time to review medical histories and offer clear, understandable healthcare plans.

  • Assessment of acute fevers, respiratory infections, and digestive ailments
  • Health guidance for adults and senior citizens
  • Medication review and regular follow-up consultations
  • Referral guidance for external specialist care when required

* The availability of specific consultation timings and treatments should be confirmed directly with the hospital before visiting.

Hospital Facilities

2. Inpatient Bed Facilities

OSG Hospital has inpatient bed facilities for patients who require admission, observation, and supervised recovery within the hospital premises.

Our inpatient rooms are designed to provide a calm, hygienic, and recuperative setting. Dedicated medical oversight ensures that patients who require continuous rest, hydration, monitoring, or medical administration are cared for attentively.

  • Supervised beds for medical observation and recovery
  • Clean, ventilated, and peaceful patient rooms
  • Doctor rounds and daily progress evaluation
  • Comfortable space for visiting family members

* Admission decisions depend on clinical assessment, bed availability, and the hospital's capabilities. Please contact the hospital to confirm admission arrangements.

3. Nursing Care Support

Nursing care supports patients during their hospital stay and recovery. Our nursing assistance includes observing patient well-being, recording vitals, supporting prescribed care plans, and assisting with appropriate patient care activities within the scope of the available nursing service.

  • Regular monitoring of blood pressure, temperature, and vitals
  • Assisting patients with mobility and comfort during admission
  • Prompt communication of any condition change to the consulting physician

4. Patient and Family Guidance

We recognise that navigating illness, medications, and hospital admissions can bring anxiety to patients and their families. Our front office and medical team provide clear explanations regarding care plans, expected recovery, and follow-up guidance.

  • Transparent discussion of diagnosis and recommended steps
  • Practical instructions for home care after discharge
  • Information on our upcoming senior living and old age care plans

Important Service Information & Clinical Boundaries

Clear communication is essential for responsible healthcare. Please review our service scope before visiting.

OSG Hospital does not currently offer its own in-house laboratory or diagnostic testing services, an in-house pharmacy, an emergency department, or an ambulance service as part of the confirmed facilities listed for this website.

Please contact the hospital to confirm current service availability and doctor timings before visiting. For any acute medical emergency, please call 108 immediately or visit an accredited emergency hospital.
