Hospital Facilities

Transparent Healthcare Information for Patients & Families

We believe in complete transparency and responsible communication so patients and families can make informed healthcare decisions.

Confirmed Facilities Available

The following facilities are verified and operational at OSG Hospital to support medical consultations and inpatient recovery:

  • General Medicine Consultations: Outpatient assessment and medical reviews.
  • Inpatient Bed Facilities: Supervised hospital beds for medical admissions.
  • Nursing Care Support: Dedicated nursing team monitoring vitals and patient welfare.
  • Registration & Reception Desk: Organized consultation registration and patient reception.
  • Patient Waiting & Guidance Area: Clean, comfortable environment for patients and accompanying relatives.
Services Not In-House (Advisory)

To ensure patient safety and honest representation, please note that the following services are not operated in-house at our premises:

  • In-House Laboratory / Diagnostic Testing: No in-house lab; external diagnostic centers can be recommended.
  • In-House Pharmacy: Prescriptions are provided for fulfillment at external licensed pharmacies.
  • Emergency & Ambulance Services: We do not operate emergency trauma or ambulance fleets. For emergencies, please call 108 immediately.
Before Visiting: Please confirm consultation timings, inpatient bed availability, and admission requirements by phone or WhatsApp before visiting.
Planned Senior Care Initiative

OSG Senior Care — A Future Home for Comfort, Dignity and Companionship

At OSG Hospital, we understand how important it is for senior citizens to have a comfortable living environment, respectful support, and assistance with everyday activities when needed.

1. Senior Accommodation

Plans for a comfortable and safe residential living environment designed around the physical needs, privacy, and accessibility requirements of older adults.

2. Meal Arrangements

Plans to provide regular, balanced meals for residents, with dietary requirements and nutritional considerations coordinated under the final service arrangements.

3. Daily Living Assistance

Plans to offer assistance with everyday activities, personal care routines, and companionship according to each resident's individual level of independence.

Planning to Care for Your Loved Ones? Register Your Early Interest

Important Notice: The OSG Old Age Home is currently in the planning stage. Accommodation, meals, and daily living assistance are proposed future services and are not yet advertised as operational. Admission dates, fees, location details, staffing, and final facilities have not yet been finalized. We welcome families exploring senior living options in Bengaluru to register their interest for updates.
