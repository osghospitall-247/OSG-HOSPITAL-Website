Patient Safety & Honest Disclosure

Services Not Currently Available at Our Premises

To help patients make informed decisions, please note that the following services are not included in the confirmed facilities for OSG Hospital:

No In-House Laboratory

OSG Hospital does not operate an in-house pathology or diagnostic testing laboratory. Doctors issue standard diagnostic referral slips so patients can obtain tests at licensed labs.

No In-House Pharmacy

We do not have an on-site retail pharmacy. Prescriptions written by our consulting physicians can be filled conveniently at nearby pharmacies across Bengaluru.

No Emergency / Ambulance

OSG Hospital does not operate emergency trauma units or an ambulance fleet. In the event of an urgent, critical emergency, please dial 108 immediately.

Contact Before Visiting

Please confirm consultation timings, inpatient bed availability, admission requirements, and nursing arrangements before visiting OSG Hospital.
