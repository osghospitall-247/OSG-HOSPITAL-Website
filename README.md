## 🏥 Hospital Overview & Services

### Confirmed In-House Facilities
- **General Medicine Consultations:** Comprehensive clinical assessment for common adult conditions, diagnostic symptom evaluation, medication management, and external specialist referrals.
- **Inpatient Bed Facilities:** Supervised, monitored hospital beds for patients requiring medical observation and recovery.
- **Professional Nursing Care:** Attentive nursing monitoring vitals, assisting patient recovery, and executing doctor care plans.
- **Front Office & Reception:** Organized patient registration and family guidance.

### Transparent Service Disclosure
- OSG Hospital **does not** operate an in-house laboratory, an in-house retail pharmacy, or an emergency/ambulance fleet on-site.
- For acute life-threatening medical emergencies, patients and families are advised to dial **108** immediately.

### Planned Initiative: OSG Senior Care
- An upcoming **Old Age Home & Senior Citizen Care** initiative in Bengaluru, currently in the *planning stage*.
- Proposed future services include comfortable accommodation, nutritious meals, daily living assistance, and compassionate companionship. Families can register their interest for upcoming announcements.

## 🚀 Features & Technical Highlights
1. **Top 5 Sliding Reviews Carousel:** Positioned right above the footer, featuring touch swipe, autoplay, pause-on-hover, and dot navigation.
